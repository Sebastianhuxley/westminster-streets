# Westminster Streets

An open-world Westminster in the browser (Three.js). Play: https://sebastianhuxley.github.io/westminster-streets/

## Rifle

You carry a Lee-Enfield Mk1, slung across your back.

- **Q**: draw or sling the rifle. Once it's drawn you're aiming: just move the mouse (the pointer is captured; press Esc to release it)
- **Left click**: fire (hold it to keep firing as fast as the bolt allows)
- **Hold the right mouse button**: zoom in
- **R**: reload

The magazine holds ten rounds. You work the bolt by hand after each shot, and reloading uses two five-round chargers. On touch screens, use the RIFLE, ZOOM, FIRE and RELOAD buttons.

Gunfire makes people scatter. Shooting someone brings the police, and shooting a police officer brings them faster.

Model: Lee-Enfield Mk1 from CGTrader, optimised into `wpn_lee_enfield.glb` (10k triangles, 1K PBR textures, with the bolt as a separate part so it can move).
